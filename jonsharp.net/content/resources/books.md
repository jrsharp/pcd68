# Recommended Books

## Assembly Language Programming

### 68000 Assembly Language Programming
**Author:** Lance Leventhal  
**Publisher:** Osborne McGraw-Hill  
Classic text covering 68000 assembly programming with practical examples and exercises.

### Programming the 68000
**Author:** Steve Williams  
**Publisher:** Sybex  
Comprehensive guide to 68000 programming including system design considerations.

## Computer Architecture

### Computer Organization and Design
**Authors:** Patterson & Hennessy  
**Publisher:** Morgan Kaufmann  
Essential text on computer architecture principles, including RISC vs CISC design.

### Structured Computer Organization
**Author:** Andrew Tanenbaum  
**Publisher:** Prentice Hall  
Multi-level approach to understanding computer systems from gates to applications.

## Retrocomputing History

### Dealers of Lightning
**Author:** Michael Hiltzik  
**Publisher:** HarperBusiness  
The story of Xerox PARC and the invention of the personal computer.

### The Soul of a New Machine
**Author:** Tracy Kidder  
**Publisher:** Back Bay Books  
Pulitzer Prize-winning account of computer development at Data General.

### Revolution in the Valley
**Author:** Andy Hertzfeld  
**Publisher:** O'Reilly Media  
First-hand account of the original Macintosh development team.

## Hardware Design

### The Art of Electronics
**Authors:** Horowitz & Hill  
**Publisher:** Cambridge University Press  
Comprehensive reference for analog and digital circuit design.

### Digital Design and Computer Architecture
**Authors:** Harris & Harris  
**Publisher:** Morgan Kaufmann  
Modern approach to digital system design and computer architecture.
