# About Jon Sharp

Jon Sharp is a software engineer and hardware enthusiast with a passion for retrocomputing and embedded systems.

## Professional Focus

- Low-level systems programming
- 68000 and vintage processor architecture
- Hardware design and reverse engineering  
- Embedded systems development
- Real-time operating systems

## Current Projects

**PCD-68 Virtual Computer**
A complete 68000-based virtual machine featuring custom hardware emulation, assembly development tools, and this very cyberterminal interface.

**Retrocomputing Research**
Active restoration and enhancement of vintage computer systems including the Canon Cat, Apple II series, and various Z80-based machines.

**Assembly Language Evangelism**
Promoting the continued relevance of assembly programming in an age of high-level abstractions.
